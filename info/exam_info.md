# Exam - General information 

There will be an **oral exam** at the end of the semester. 

The first examination will take place on **August 03, 2026** and **August 04, 2026**. 
The second examination date is **September 02, 2026** and **September 03, 2026**.

Please register for the oral exam in Causal Machine Learning in the Student Portal ([*Studierendenportal*](https://studierende.hhu.de)) during the [exam registration period (**May 18, 2026 to June 5, 2026**)](https://www.wiwi.hhu.de/fileadmin/redaktion/Fakultaeten/Wirtschaftswissenschaftliche_Fakultaet/Dekanat/Pruefungsausschuss/Klausurtermine/Anmeldetermine_WiWi_WS_2025-26_und_SoSe_2026.pdf). Only one exam date is listed in the Student Portal. Register for this date if you plan to take the exam on either the first examination date (August 03/04) or the second examination date (September 02/03). \
Please also note that the **withdrawal deadline** for the exam (regardless of whether you intend to take it on the **first** or **second date**) is **July 26, 2026**.
